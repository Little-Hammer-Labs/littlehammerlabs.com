Questions

Straight answers before the call.

Is it really free?

Yes. The 45-minute audit and the one-page plan are free, and the plan is yours to keep whether or not you ever work with us.

Will you try to sell me something?

You get a straight recommendation: do it yourself, have us build it, or leave it alone. If leaving it alone is the right call, we will say so.

Do I have to switch software?

No. We build around the tools you already use, even if that is paper and a spreadsheet.

Who will I talk to?

Both founders, Max and Ryan. The people on your audit call are the people who build it.

Do you come to us?

Your choice: on site or by video, whichever is easier for you.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing formal. It helps to know the software you use today, your biggest office headache, and how a job goes from first call to paid invoice.

What happens to my data?

You own everything we build. We can run systems on your own equipment so your data never leaves your building, or use private cloud instances with no data sharing.

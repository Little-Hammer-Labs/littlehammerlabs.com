02Owners

Thinking about selling?

If you've built a services business over a couple of decades and you're starting to think about what comes next, we'd like a conversation. No broker required, no obligation, and we'll tell you quickly if we're not the right fit.

What we're looking for is in the thesis. Roughly: $500K to $3M in revenue, a few years of steady earnings, repeat customers, and an office that works harder than it should. Southern California first.

What we offer a seller

  • A straight answer in two weeks. We'll look at your numbers and tell you whether we're a buyer and at what range.
  • Your name and your people stay. We don't rebrand and we don't walk in with a layoff list.
  • Flexibility on terms. A clean exit, a slow transition, or a seller note that pays you more over time. We've seen all three work.
  • The people you meet are the buyers. Max and Ryan do the diligence, sign the papers, and show up on day one.

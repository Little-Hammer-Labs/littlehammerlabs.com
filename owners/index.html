03Process

How it works.

Five steps. The first one costs you half an hour and no paperwork.

  1. A 30-minute call

    You tell us about the business and what you want next. No documents needed.

  2. A look at the numbers

    Under NDA, you share a few years of financials. We tell you within two weeks whether we're a fit.

  3. A proposal in plain English

    Price, structure, timeline, and what happens to your team.

  4. Diligence and closing

    Usually 60 to 90 days, with your attorney and CPA alongside ours.

  5. Transition

    You stay on as long as the agreement says, paid, while we take over the day to day.

05FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Do I need a business broker?

No. You can work with us directly, and we encourage you to have your own attorney and CPA review any offer.

Will my employees find out?

Not from us. Everything stays confidential until you decide when and how to tell your team.

What is seller financing, and is it safe for me?

Instead of all cash at closing, part of the price is paid to you over time with interest. It's secured by the business, documented by attorneys, and often lets you get a higher total price and spread out taxes. Your CPA can tell you how it fits your situation.

What happens to my name and my customers?

The business keeps its name. You introduce us to key customers during the transition.

How do you decide what my business is worth?

Mostly on consistent earnings over the last few years, how dependent the business is on you, and how steady the customer base is. We explain the range and how we got there.

I'm not ready to sell for a few years. Should I still call?

Yes. The best transitions are planned two to three years out, and the Systems Audit is a good place to start.

What if you're not the right buyer?

We'll tell you quickly, and if we can, point you to someone who is.

What size businesses do you buy?

Roughly $500K to $3M in revenue and $100K to $750K in owner earnings, in San Diego County first.

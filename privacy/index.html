Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

What we collect

When you request the investor memo, book a call, or email us about a business, we collect your name, email address, and whatever you choose to tell us. The investor form also asks whether you are an accredited investor and for an indicative check size. We use this to decide what to send you and to follow up.

How we use it

To reply to you, send the materials you asked for, and keep in touch about Little Hammer Labs. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing.

Who handles it

Form submissions are processed by Formspree and delivered to our email. Calls are scheduled through Cal.com. Email is hosted on Google Workspace. Each of those providers has its own privacy policy and sees only what you submit to it.

Business owners

If you share financial or operational information about a company you are considering selling, we treat it as confidential, use it only to evaluate the opportunity, and will sign a non-disclosure agreement before you send anything sensitive if you ask.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. An ad blocker or browser setting will opt you out.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. Email contact@littlehammerlabs.com and we will respond within 30 days.
